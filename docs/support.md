## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**What is D426 Mastery?**
D426 Mastery is an iOS study app designed to help Western Governors University (WGU) students pass the D426 Data Management Foundations Objective Assessment. It includes flashcards with spaced repetition, quiz questions, a SQL playground, and more.

**Is D426 Mastery affiliated with WGU?**
No. D426 Mastery is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Western Governors University.

**Does the App work offline?**
Yes. D426 Mastery is 100% offline capable. All content and your study progress are stored locally on your device.

2. Tap your **Apple ID** at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Find **D426 Mastery** and tap **Cancel Subscription**

**How do I request a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/).

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open the App, go to **More > Premium**, and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### Study Features

**How does spaced repetition work?**
D426 Mastery uses the SM-2 algorithm (similar to Anki). After reviewing a flashcard, you rate your recall. The App schedules the next review based on your rating — cards you struggle with appear more frequently, while mastered cards appear less often.

**What topics are covered?**
All D426 competencies:
- Relational Model Concepts
- ER Diagrams & Crow's Foot Notation
- Primary & Foreign Keys
- Normalization (1NF through BCNF)
- SQL DDL Commands
- SQL DML Commands
- Conceptual, Logical & Physical Data Models
- DBMS Architecture

**Can I run real SQL queries?**
Yes. The SQL Playground lets you write and execute real SQL queries against sample databases using SQLite. Results display in a formatted table.

**What sample databases are available?**
Five realistic databases: University, Sales, Hospital, Employee, and Inventory. Each includes full schemas and sample data.

### Technical Issues

**My progress was lost. Can I recover it?**
Study data is stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the App, progress cannot be recovered. We recommend keeping the App installed until you pass your exam.

**The App is crashing. What should I do?**
1. Make sure you're running the latest version of the App
2. Restart your iPhone
3. Delete and reinstall the App (note: this will reset your progress)
4. If the issue persists, email us at support@ellasid.com with your iOS version and a description of the problem

**I found an error in a flashcard or quiz question.**
Please email us at support@ellasid.com with the specific question or card text and the correction. We review and update content regularly.
